PokerFun hand verifier

Paste the record of a hand you played. This page checks, step by step, how its cards were dealt and how it was played and paid. It runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere.

What this checks

  • The deck was a complete deck of 52 cards, re-locked and reordered by three separate dealer programs in turn, each with a valid proof that its output is a re-encryption and permutation of its input.
  • Where each card went, the board included, was decided by public randomness that did not exist yet when the hand was sealed.
  • Your own two cards are the two sealed to the device you played on. The board and every hand shown at the table are the cards at their places in the locked deck, opened by all three dealers, each street only after the betting before it had closed.
  • Every move a device signed checks against the key the hand names for its seat, over the board that device was shown. The forced bets and the checks and folds the table played for a seat are marked apart, and no device signed them as moves. The recorded betting followed the published rules, and the pots and who won them are what the betting and the cards give.
  • With your receipt: the record you are checking is the one PokerFun signed, and it has not been edited since. A hand that was started and abandoned appears as a voided hand, not as a gap.

What it does not prove

  • This way of dealing has not yet been reviewed by an outside cryptographer.
  • PokerFun runs all three dealers. Reading a hidden card takes all three together, so it is harder for anyone inside PokerFun, not impossible.
  • The proofs show that each dealer's output is its input re-locked and reordered, not how the dealer chose the order. That nobody knows which card is where rests on at least one dealer keeping its own order and key secret; that the deal is fair rests on the drand round being unknown when the hand was sealed.
  • It does not show that a dealer waited out a seat's turn clock before taking a check or fold the table played for that seat: each dealer keeps that rule by its own clock, which the record does not carry.
  • It works out the pots from the record. It does not show that PokerFun then moved those chips.
  • It does not show you other players' folded or mucked cards. Those stay hidden, as they do at a real table.
  • Without the log from your own device it checks that the record is consistent. Only a device that was connected at the time can confirm the hand was fixed before the randomness was published.
  • It cannot tell whether other players shared their cards with each other.
  • It checks a record's place in the log only against the root that came with it, and does not look for that root among roots kept anywhere else. A record's place in the log shows what PokerFun's server signed, not that the log was never rewritten.
  • Every hand is dealt from its public drand round, or not at all. A record that says otherwise fails here.
  • It checks hands dealt under the rules of revision 3, which every PokerFun hand has used since 2026-10-15, and no other.

The hand to check

Paste the record you downloaded from the table (it starts with a curly bracket), or choose the file. A file with several hands is checked hand by hand, and the links between hands of one table are checked too.

Compare with a root of the log you already have (optional)

A root of PokerFun's log of hands you kept or found published, such as a day's root: the block of text with "size", "root" and "sig". If its size is not the size of the root the record is under, also paste the proof between the two, which PokerFun's site serves at /api/fairness/consistency?from=<the smaller size>&to=<the larger>. The page then checks that both are roots of one log that only grew.

How to know this page itself is genuine

This verifier is one file, built from its source by a script anyone can run. PokerFun does not yet publish the file's fingerprint (SHA-256) in a place it does not control; until it does, a saved copy can be compared with one someone you trust built from the source. You can also open the saved copy with your network switched off: it works the same.

The curve arithmetic and every signature check use noble-curves 1.9.7, an audited open source library, included unchanged inside this file.
